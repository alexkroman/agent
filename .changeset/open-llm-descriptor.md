**One LLM factory, open vocabularies, widened constants, and an experimental lane.** Breaking.

- **`llm({ provider, model, baseUrl?, apiKeyEnv?, providerOptions? })` replaces every per-vendor LLM factory.** `anthropicLlm`, `openAILlm`, `googleLlm`, `mistralLlm`, `xAILlm`, `groqLlm`, `cerebrasLlm`, `openRouterLlm`, `gatewayLlm` and `assemblyAILlm` are removed, with their options types (`AnthropicLlmOptions` … `AssemblyAILlmOptions`, `ModelOptions`) and the exported base URLs (`ASSEMBLYAI_LLM_GATEWAY_URL`, `ASSEMBLYAI_LLM_GATEWAY_EU_URL`, `OPENROUTER_BASE_URL`, `CEREBRAS_BASE_URL`). Write `llm({ provider: "anthropic", model: "…" })`. `provider` is `KnownLlmProvider | (string & {})`: a provider with no built-in entry resolves as an OpenAI-compatible endpoint when the descriptor names a `baseUrl` (key from `apiKeyEnv`, else `<PROVIDER>_API_KEY`), and `aai build` warns about one with neither. Key variables and base URLs now live in `@alexkroman1/aai-runtime`'s resolver table. `model` is required everywhere; the string shorthand (`llm: "model-id"`, `llm: "creator/model"`) and omitting `llm` are unchanged.
- **AssemblyAI options moved into `providerOptions`:** `region` and `reasoningEffort` are `providerOptions: { region, reasoningEffort }` (`AssemblyAILlmProviderOptions`), and `gatewayUrl` is `baseUrl`. The descriptor's options shape changed accordingly.
- **`providerOptions` on an OpenAI-compatible provider (`openrouter`, `cerebras`, any `baseUrl` provider) is merged into the request body**, verbatim, instead of being passed as the AI SDK's `providerOptions.openai` — whose schema stripped every vendor key, so `{ provider: { order } }` or `{ top_k }` was silently dropped. A field the SDK writes itself (`model`, `messages`, `tools`, `stream`, a set call setting) wins a collision. Native providers are unchanged: their `providerOptions` remain that client's typed AI SDK entry.
- **Open name lists.** `AssemblyAIGatewayModel` is `KnownGatewayModel | (string & {})` (the generated snapshot is `KnownGatewayModel` on `/llm`), `VoicePresetName` is `KnownVoicePresetName | (string & {})`, and `turnDetection` is `TurnDetectionMode` (`KnownTurnDetectionMode | (string & {})`). Unknown values compile, are accepted by the config schema, and are warned about by `agentConfigWarnings` (`aai build`/`aai dev`) instead of being refused; an unknown preset emits no text and an unknown turn-detection mode runs as `"auto"`.
- **Published constants are typed as their primitive, not their literal value:** `ASSEMBLYAI_LLM_DEFAULT_MODEL: AssemblyAIGatewayModel`, `DEFAULT_SYSTEM_PROMPT: string`, `VOICE_PRESETS: Readonly<Record<KnownVoicePresetName, string>>`, `ASSEMBLYAI_TTS_DEFAULT_VOICE: AssemblyAITtsVoiceId`, and `: number`/`: string` on the timeout, window, sample-rate, endpoint and default-voice constants of `/step`, `/step-files`, `/step-errors`, `/channels`, `/stt`, `/tts`, `/coding-tools` and the root. A prompt's or default's VALUE is a behaviour, not a type to pin.
- **New `@alexkroman1/aai/experimental` subpath** — the lane a new, unmeasured feature ships in before promotion, outside the capability contracts and the rendered reference. First occupant: `openAICompatibleLlm({ provider, model, baseUrl, apiKeyEnv })`.
